About

What is Hack Club?

Hack Club is a global nonprofit network of student-led coding & maker clubs. It gives teenagers mentorship, resources, and sometimes funding or hardware to build real projects, run their own clubs, and meet other young makers around the world.

What is Caspian Hack Club?

Caspian Hack Club is a Hack Club–approved club bringing that movement to Azerbaijan — open to teens roughly aged 13–18, focused on hands-on hardware: PCBs, microcontrollers, and open-source builds rather than just software.

What We Do

Custom PCBs

Designing and fabricating boards from schematic to silkscreen. No black-box modules.

Microcontrollers

Open Source

Every schematic, board file, and line of firmware pushed publicly. Fork it, fix it, ship it.
